# The Plantain Circle · El Círculo de Plátano

A simple field guide for growing stronger plantains, with better soil and better water.

Made for the people of the Darién, Panama. A public good from Plantain Goods.

## What this is

The plantain circle is a permaculture way of planting. You dig a pit in the middle, fill it with organic matter, and plant six plantains around it on a raised ring of soil. The pit feeds the plants and holds water through the dry months.

This guide shows how to build one, step by step, in English and Spanish side by side. It covers choosing a site, picking healthy suckers, building the circle, the first ninety days, care through the seasons, and companion plants. Five illustrations go with it.

The pattern is being tried as a test planting in the Darién with the Wounaan community.

## How to use it

- **Read it online:** open the guide in any web browser, on a phone or a computer.
- **Print it:** download `plantain-circle.pdf` and print as many copies as you need.
- **Take it to the field:** once the page has loaded, it keeps working without internet.

## How you can help

This is a working draft. It gets better as people use it in the field.

- **Tell us how it went.** If you build a circle from this guide, share your site, your results, and what you would change.
- **Translate it.** Woun Meu, Kreyòl, French, and Portuguese versions are all welcome.
- **Add local plant names.** The companion plants are listed by their Spanish names. Wounaan and other local names would make the guide more useful.
- **Draw for it.** New or extra illustrations in the same warm sepia, engraved style are welcome, shared on the same terms.

To send feedback, open an "Issue" on this page. If you're comfortable with GitHub, you can also suggest changes directly. Or just get in touch (below).

## Putting your own copy online

The guide is one web page with no special setup, so any free website host can put it online in a few minutes. Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, and GitHub Pages all work. Copy this folder and point the host at it.

## Thanks

The plantain circle comes from the work of Bill Mollison, the founder of permaculture, and Geoff Lawton, whose teaching in the tropics has refined it over decades. This guide adapts their work to the Darién's climate and to the knowledge of the Wounaan community.
